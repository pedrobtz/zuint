# zuint bug hunt, 2026-11-27

Commit `f07c9e4`. Hunters: 4 on Fable 5.2, one at a time, slices:
decimal128 through its R layer; the integer types' R layer; composition
with bit64, vctrs, dplyr and data.table; the Arrow bridge. Coordinator on
Opus 5.5. Built here (macOS x86_64): the native `__int128` build and
zufast's portable fallback (`ZUF_NO_INT128`); every hunter ran its edge
cases on both, and they agreed byte for byte.
Unverified: big-endian (`zu_read16`/`zu_write16`), 32-bit, Windows, long
vectors above 2^31 elements, `release` from another thread, and
sanitizers or valgrind on these paths (CI runs those on the suite).

Every claim below was re-run by the coordinator on a fresh install of the
pinned commit (`2026-11-27-bug-hunt-probes/rerun/`); all 36 reproduced.
They consolidate into 20 issues by root cause.

The probe scripts are in `2026-11-27-bug-hunt-probes/`, one directory per
hunter. They read the library from `ZLIB` (or the scratch path written in
their preamble), so run them with `ZLIB=<a library holding zuint at
f07c9e4>`.

## 1. Make mean() the correctly rounded exact mean — #148, medium, reproduced
- Where: [`src/zu_reduce.c:255-291`](https://github.com/pedrobtz/zuint/blob/f07c9e436f972674b24b39aa397514a3d3e5a275/src/zu_reduce.c#L255-L291)
- Trigger: `mean(decimal128(c(rep("0.02", 6e5), rep("-0.03", 399999)), 5, 2))`
  is 25,541 ulps from DuckDB's `avg()`; `mean(int128(c(0, 2, 3)))` is
  1.6666666666666665; three copies of 9007199254740993 have a mean above
  `max()`, so `summary()` prints Mean above Max.
- Cause: the 64-bit path rounds the sum then divides; the 128-bit path adds
  `r / m` to a rounded quotient and cancels.
- Fix: divide exactly, round once.
- Family: 1. Siblings checked: `median()` shares the routine; both paths fail.
- Test the suite lacked: mean() against gmp's exact rational, at most half
  an ulp, with small totals and large n.

## 2. match(), %in% and table() against a bare double compare text — #149, medium, reproduced
- Where: [`R/reduce.R:157-170`](https://github.com/pedrobtz/zuint/blob/f07c9e436f972674b24b39aa397514a3d3e5a275/R/reduce.R#L157-L170), [`R/decimal.R:417-426`](https://github.com/pedrobtz/zuint/blob/f07c9e436f972674b24b39aa397514a3d3e5a275/R/decimal.R#L417-L426)
- Trigger: `int64(100000) %in% 100000` is FALSE; `match(int64(1), 1)` is NA
  under `scipen = -5`; the decimal 0.1 matches the double 0.1 though `==`
  says they differ.
- Cause: base `match()` sends only the classed side through `mtfrm()`.
- Fix: not by a method; document it and point to `zu_match()`.
- Family: 4. Siblings: `intersect()`, `setdiff()`, `union()`, `merge()`.
- Test the suite lacked: match against 1e5, 1e15, 0.1, TRUE, and under a
  hostile session.

## 3. A bare integer64 read as raw doubles when bit64 is not loaded — #150, medium, reproduced
- Where: [`R/ops.R:43-44`](https://github.com/pedrobtz/zuint/blob/f07c9e436f972674b24b39aa397514a3d3e5a275/R/ops.R#L43-L44) and six more sites (the issue lists them).
- Trigger: `int64(1) & as_integer64(int64(-1))` is NA; `vec_cast()` of an
  integer64 NA is a false lossy error; `zu_match()` finds NA at -1.
- Cause: base `is.na()`/`as.logical()` on an `integer64` argument read the bits.
- Family: 3. Siblings: seven sites in `&`/`|`, `zu_match()`, `vec_cast()`
  (two), decimal `==`, decimal `zu_match()`, `zu_ifelse()`.
- Test the suite lacked: the bit64 parity processes with a bare integer64
  operand.

## 4. integer64 * uint64, / and ^ on raw bits when bit64 is loaded — #151, medium, reproduced
- Where: [`R/bit64.R:132-145`](https://github.com/pedrobtz/zuint/blob/f07c9e436f972674b24b39aa397514a3d3e5a275/R/bit64.R#L132-L145)
- Trigger: `as.integer64(c(-2, 3)) * uint64(5)` is `0 0`.
- Cause: bit64's `is.double()` is TRUE for a classed REALSXP without a method.
- Fix: register `is.double.zu_uint64` in bit64's namespace (verified by the hunter).
- Family: 3. Siblings: `zu_int64` escapes through `is.double.integer64`;
  the 128-bit types are complex.

## 5. Complex group functions corrupt the zuint types — #152, medium, reproduced
- Trigger: `Conj(int128(5))` is -170141183460469231731687303715884105723;
  `Re()`, `Mod()` of a decimal or int128 cannot be printed.
- Family: 3. Siblings: found on decimal128 by hunter 1; int128 and uint128
  by the coordinator.

## 6. A zuint count in rep(), diff() and head() reads as raw bits — #153, medium, reproduced
- Trigger: `rep(int64(1:3), times = int64(2))` is `int64(0)`.
- Family: 3. Siblings: indexing converts; `round(x, int64(-2))` converts.

## 7. Recognise DuckDB's hugeint extension by parsing its JSON — #154, medium, reproduced
- Where: [`src/zu_arrow.c:88-134`](https://github.com/pedrobtz/zuint/blob/f07c9e436f972674b24b39aa397514a3d3e5a275/src/zu_arrow.c#L88-L134)
- Trigger: `vendor_name` "Other", or a nested `type_name`, reads as int128
  where DuckDB reads BLOB; valid metadata with the key's text earlier as a
  value falls back to raw; a decimal under any `arrow.opaque` reads as
  int128 without `to =`.
- Family: 4.

## 8. INT32_MIN under to = int64() — #155, medium, reproduced
- Cause: the column passes through an R integer, whose NA is INT32_MIN.
- Family: 1.

## 9. sscanf overflow in the d:p,s parser — #156, medium, reproduced (test entry point)
- Trigger: `d:5,4294967296` is accepted as int128-readable; signed overflow
  in `sscanf("%d")` is undefined behaviour.
- Family: 4.

## 10. round() and signif() with a fractional or infinite digits — #157, medium, reproduced
- Trigger: `round(int64(1234), -2.7)` is 1200 (base 1000); `round(x, Inf)`
  is an unclassed error. Found by hunters 1 and 2 independently.

## 11. x ^ y past 2^128 leaves the type; NA ^ 0 — #158, medium, reproduced
- Trigger: `int64(3)^2^128` is a double `Inf`; `NA_int64_^0L` is NA where
  base and bit64 give 1.

## 12. seq_zu() and seq() — #159, medium, reproduced
- Trigger: `seq_zu(int64(1), int64(10), length.out = 3)` is 1 2 3;
  `seq(int64(10), int64(1))` and `seq(int64(5))` are refused.

## 13. %*% depends on whether bit64 is loaded — #160, medium, reproduced
- Trigger: `int64(1:2) %*% int64(1:2)` is 0 without bit64, 5 with it.
- Family: 3 (allowlisted as a mask; it is an S3 method).

## 14. Set functions fail with bit64 attached — #161, medium, reproduced
- Trigger: `library(bit64); setdiff(int64(1:3), int64(1))` errors in `as()`.

## 15. Padding by data-frame row growth and data.table — #162, medium, reproduced
- Trigger: `d[5, "id"] <- 7L` pads rows 3-4 with 9218868437227407266;
  data.table pads uint64 and the 128-bit types alike and groups the uint64 NA
  with its neighbour.
- Family: 2. No zuint method is reached; bit64 has the base case too.

## 16-19. Suggestions — #163 to #166, low, reproduced
- #163: arguments ignored or mishandled (`na.rm = 1`, `index.return`,
  `incomparables`, `big.interval`, `lag = 0`, `nomatch`, `print(quote =)`,
  `print(max =)`, `big.mark` under `OutDec`).
- #164: names dropped by `abs()`, `sign()`, `!` and the double fallback.
- #165: the base functions that read the payload (`c()` with a non-zuint
  first argument, `is.unsorted()`, `cut()`, `complete.cases()`,
  `aggregate()`, `rowsum()`, `var()`, indexing by a zuint), documented,
  with a `cut()` method.
- #166: unclassed errors from the Arrow handler on a released schema; a
  silent type change past 4096 metadata entries.

## 20. Should to = int128()/uint128() cast, and lossy = "error" govern a to = cast? — #167, question
Design §9.4 decides the current behaviour; the consequence is a silent
negative int128 for `to = uint128()` on DuckDB's default UHUGEINT export.

## Cross-cutting

- **Composition with an unclassed or foreign operand** (#149, #150, #151,
  #153, #165): eight findings share one shape. zuint's methods are exact on
  zuint operands, and the bug appears where the other operand is a bare
  double, a bare `integer64`, an R integer count, or where base R strips
  the class. The suite tests zuint against zuint and against base vectors
  as the left operand; the other side of each composition was untested.
  Candidate rule for `correctness.md`: *for every method, test each
  argument as each foreign class the package claims to accept (bare
  double, logical, the sibling package's class with that package loaded
  and not), and the method's class in second position.*
- **A numeric result's last bit** (#148): an exactness guarantee for
  integers did not carry over to their means. A double result needs a
  correctly-rounded oracle, not a tolerance.
- **Parity allowlists hide methods** (#160, #161): bit64's `%*%` and set
  functions were assumed to be masks; one is an S3 method, the others need
  S4 `as()`. An allowlist entry needs the same evidence as a test.

## Held: what was probed and stood

- Decimal arithmetic, rounding, casts, parsing and comparison against
  DuckDB 2.0.1 and gmp: about 26,000 double casts, 400 random type pairs,
  19 rounding digits on 16 types, 30,000 strings, 6,000 × 17 comparisons; 0
  differences, both builds.
- Integer arithmetic, the overflow policies, reductions, parsing (decimal,
  hex, binary), formatting round trips, the bit functions, `seq_zu()`
  values, hashing and ordering against gmp; both builds.
- vctrs (every verb listed in hunter 3's report), dplyr joins and padding,
  pillar, bit64 caches and exported functions on `zu_int64`, `int64` in
  data.table, serialisation round trips.
- Arrow import over 1,830 framings (offsets, bitmaps, `null_count`) and
  export over 1,260 checks read by nanoarrow, DuckDB and arrow; sentinels
  under every lossy policy; release counts and moves; the metadata reader
  at its cap and malformed lengths.
- The hostile session (`LC_ALL=C`, `OutDec`, `scipen`, `digits`, `TZ`)
  changes nothing but #149 and #163's `big.mark` warning.

## Not reached

- Long vectors, big-endian, threads, allocation failure, sanitizers on
  these paths; tidyr; `as.Date`/`as.POSIXct` beyond trivial epochs;
  `to = bit64::integer64()` and zuarrow's `arrow_matrix()`/`arrow_next()`;
  the C table `zuint_api_v1` (covered by `tools/fixed`).

## Not reproduced, and by design

- All 36 claims reproduced.
- By design: Summary dispatching on its first argument (#125, documented);
  exact comparison of a decimal with a double (`decimal128("0.1",5,1) ==
  0.1` is FALSE, design §2.2, §5); the division family in double (§2.2);
  `decimal128(x, 1)` on a DECIMAL(5,2) cutting the scale too, since a scale
  cannot exceed its precision; `min()` of nothing returning a double `Inf`
  (§0.1); the metadata entry cap (CLAUDE.md), filed only for its silent
  type change (#166); the opaque handler casting nothing (§9.4), filed as a
  question (#167).
- In zuarrow, not zuint: a list column of uint64 or the 128-bit types
  cannot be written, and nested wide integers do not come back as zuint
  types (hunter 4 reproduced the first with a toy class and zuint not
  loaded).
